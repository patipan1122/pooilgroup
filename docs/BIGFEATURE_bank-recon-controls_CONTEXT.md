## Feature bundle (7 asks)
1. ปุ่ม "นำออกทั้งหมด / ย้อนกลับทั้งหมด" (bulk undo) on reconcile page.
2. "คลังเก็บของที่แมชแล้ว" — searchable archive of matched/confirmed items, revert even after confirm. Iridescent must revert too.
3. สิทธิ์ "ขออนุมัติแก้" — approval flow to revert a CONFIRMED match.
4. Statement (left) — show MUCH more bank detail from CSV (counterparty, who-transferred, full description).
5. รอยืนยัน (right) — full human-readable IV-level detail instead of bare tag.
6. รอยืนยัน — add filters + search (same as match tab) + show both-side totals + diff.
7. New "โยกเงิน" (internal transfer) page + a special-items hub (transfers + skipped-no-match w/ reason) across ALL accounts, beside รอยืนยัน.

### Schema (satang = ฿×100)
- `ledger_bank_txn`: bank statement rows. match_state ∈ unmatched|suggested|confirmed|excluded. Stores ref1, ref2, description, channel, balance_satang, value_date, **raw_row_json (full CSV row!)**. ← bank detail already stored, just not displayed.
- `ledger_revenue_entry`: revenue book. match_state ∈ unmatched|matched|excluded. Has description, customer_name, source_type (TRCLOUD_IV/CHAIROPS/FUELOS/...), payment_channel, source_ref, bank_txn_id, channel_code, gl_account, gl_state.
- `ledger_expense` / `ledger_payment` / `ledger_payment_request`: expense/payment book sides.
- `ledger_bank_match_group` + `ledger_bank_match_item`: N:M match (PEAK parity). group.status ∈ suggested|confirmed|reversed; reversed_by/at/reason cols exist. item.kind ∈ bank|book; book_type ∈ revenue|expense|payment|payment_request.
- `ledger_bank_match` (old 1:1): match_type incl 'exclusion'; status suggested|confirmed|reversed; exclusion_reason, reversal_reason cols.
- `ledger_bank_provisional_gl`: unmatched-credit suspense GL.
- No dedicated TRANSFER table. No approval/request-to-edit table.

### Bank adapters
- `lib/ledger/bank-adapters/types.ts` NormalizedRow: txnDate, valueDate, amountSatang, balanceSatang, ref1, ref2, description, channel, rowIndex, **rawRow (full CSV cols)**. All persisted incl raw_row_json. Counterparty/who-transferred lives in ref2/description/rawRow already.
